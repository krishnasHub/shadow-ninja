Memory · colour over time

How he looks as he remembers, floor by floor. Every row shows each movement twice: first in shadow, then under a lamp. One colour, one thing: the scarf, and only in shadow. Floor by floor the colour climbs from the tip toward his neck and deepens from maroon to a full dark red, until the whole scarf is dark red. Under a lamp it's always plain grey. At the last step the Evil Co mark appears on his chest, in grey, like everything else.

Speech · four ways

The same scene four ways. The script is shorter and half-heard, since he's hiding and dazed: faint words are ones he doesn't quite catch. The guards gesture (a point at the empty machine, a shrug, a point to where the thief went), so the body tells half of it, the way Limbo would.

Moving a desk · Floor 5

Hold E (controller X) at a desk's end and he grips it; move to push or pull. He faces the desk the whole time, hands on its end. Pushing, he leans into it and digs in; pulling, he leans back and steps backwards. Shift moves it slowly and quietly; otherwise the legs scrape. Let go of E and he lets go; ↓ and he tucks in behind it. Ducked behind it, ↓ + E grips it low: crouched behind the desk, both hands up on its top, he shuffles it along, hidden (30 px/s, silent; a camera sees a desk move).

Fighting · Floor 6

He fights like a ninja: low, quick, stepping in - MMA strikes and jiu-jitsu. The watcher is a brawler: no torch, big and square, fists up. Fight mode: X / left click strikes, Y / right click blocks. One press is a strike at random (jab, cross, body kick, leg kick, push kick), each with its own reaction. Three or more quick presses is a special, one of eight at random so a fight stays fresh (spinning back kick, flying knee, scissor takedown, superman punch, beatdown, spinning sweep, backflip kick, vault and back kick). Jump hops back (a short hop: a fight keeps it short); jump toward him and he goes over: hands on his shoulders, a flip, and the watcher spins round to face him. When he winds up (the white ticks over his head), block in time and you counter: parry, palm. Too late and he lands it: the meter jumps and he shouts. There's no health bar: he fades from near black to a dull grey as he's worn down. Dazed, the next press finishes it: clinch, knee, sweep, one more on the floor. A fight in the light gets you caught anyway.
