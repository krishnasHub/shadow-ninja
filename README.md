# Shadow Ninja

A greyscale 2D stealth side-scroller. You're a ninja inside a high-security office tower, and you have to get out. Light is the enemy: lamps, moonlight and every guard's torch fill your detection meter, and shadow drains it. On Floor 7 the guards cut the lights, and you go by sound alone.

He's also lost his memory. The clues he finds and the talk he overhears are mostly scrawl, with only numbers and a few key words clear, and more becomes readable as he remembers. The game is being rebuilt floor by floor toward 10 floors and about 4 hours of play; Floor 1 is done (about 10 minutes is the target), and the others still have their earlier, shorter layouts.

**Next up:** finish playtesting Floor 1's quiet checkpoints, which switch on after a third catch near one (playtest started, none switched on yet). See "Start here" in `TODO.md`.

The rule the whole game is built on: **the light you see is the light that catches you.** Drawing and detection use the same light geometry.

## Two versions

| Folder | What | Run it |
|---|---|---|
| `godot/` | **The game** (Godot 4.7): the story, seven floors, Godot 2D lights with hard-edged cones, menus, keyboard or Xbox controller | Double-click `godot/run.bat` (fullscreen), or open `godot/project.godot` in Godot and press F5 |
| `index.html` | The original prototype, **frozen** at phase A (no story; its own three floors): one HTML file, canvas 2D | Open it in a browser (serve it over http for the sounds) |

## Controls

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| A D / ← → | run (loud) |
| hold Shift | sneak (silent) |
| Space / W / ↑ | jump · grab a duct · land on a guard to put him to sleep |
| S / ↓ | crawl · hide behind furniture · drop from a duct |
| Ctrl | throw mode: aim with the mouse, click to throw a star (the stars come back after Floor 3; nothing to throw before that) |
| hold E | grip a desk: move to push or pull it, Shift for slow and quiet, ↓ to let go and hide behind it (after Floor 4) |
| ↓ + hold E | behind a desk: move it and stay hidden (slow; a camera that sees a desk move raises the alarm) |
| left click / E / F (controller X) | by a watcher (Floor 6 on): strike; three quick presses for a special. Finish him once he's dazed |
| right click / Q (controller Y) | in a fight: block his swing while he winds up (the ticks over his head). Jump hops back; jump toward him goes over him |
| V | Floor 7 only, for now: switch how the guards show in the dark (A: only their eyes · B: vibrating) |
| E (controller X) | take a clue · try a keypad or a safe |
| Esc (controller Start), then Tab (controller L1 / R1) | the map, then the clues you've found, then the main menu |
| R · F11 | back to the last checkpoint · fullscreen (Godot) |
| M (controller: Y in a menu) | music on / off |

Music is original, generated by `tools/make_music.py` (numpy): a calm base, a tension layer that follows danger, a drum-driven layer that comes in during a fight, and near silence on Floor 7, where the footsteps carry it. Looks and animations are prototyped first in `tools/characters.html` (open it in a browser). `TODO.md` has the design notes, status and the code map.
